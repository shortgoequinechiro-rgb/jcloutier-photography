About the Artist

The West, as I see it

Hi, I'm Jessie Cloutier. Living out West, I'm endlessly inspired by its raw beauty — the open skies, weathered landscapes, and untamed spirit that define this place.

My work is about capturing the true West: not just the scenery, but the stories, the grit, and the quiet moments that often go unnoticed. Wild, honest, and full of soul.

— Jessie
