Collector Guide

Before you order

The online print gallery shows the current products, sizes, finishes, and pricing. These notes can help you choose with confidence.

Where can I see current options and pricing?

Open the Pixieset print gallery and select a photograph. The store displays the products, sizes, finishes, and current pricing Jessie has made available.

What if I need help choosing?

Contact Jessie with the photograph you love, the approximate wall space or print width, your delivery location, and any finish you already have in mind. A quick room photo is welcome but never required.

Will the print look exactly like my screen?

Screens create light while prints reflect it, so brightness and color can vary slightly by display and room lighting. Jessie can help choose a finish that suits where the piece will live.

What about licensing or a custom project?

Print purchases do not include commercial reproduction rights. Use the contact page for publication, brand, editorial, or custom photography requests.
