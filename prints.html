Collector Guide

Before you order

A few simple details help Jessie recommend an option that fits the photograph and your space.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Share the photograph title, the approximate wall space or print width, your delivery location, and whether you already have a finish in mind. A quick room photo is welcome but never required.

How do I identify a photograph?

Open any image in the gallery. Its title appears directly below the enlarged photograph and is automatically included when you select “Inquire About This Print.”

Will the print look exactly like my screen?

Screens create light while prints reflect it, so brightness and color can vary slightly by display and room lighting. Jessie can help choose a finish that suits where the piece will live.

What about licensing or a custom project?

Print purchases do not include commercial reproduction rights. Use the contact page for publication, brand, editorial, or custom photography requests.
